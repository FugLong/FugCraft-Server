║        FugCraft Server Rules           ║

1. Be Respectful
   No harassment, discrimination, or hate speech

2. No Griefing or Stealing
   Respect land claims and builds

3. No Cheating
   No hacked clients, X-ray, or exploits
   Report bugs to staff

4. No Spam
   No excessive caps, spam, or advertising

5. No Lag Machines
   Keep farms reasonable and redstone minimal

6. Listen to Staff
   Staff decisions are final

7. Use Mods Responsibly
   Don't abuse ReMorphed or other features

Violations may result in warnings or bans.
Enjoy your time on FugCraft!
